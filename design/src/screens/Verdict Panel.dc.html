Draining a stateful queue consumer during cutover

You are judging this case whole, not in parts.
The case Read it whole. Your verdict is on the case, not on a part of it.

Problem framing

Consumers hold in-flight leases on messages. A naive cutover either drops what the old consumer was holding or hands the same message to both, and neither failure is visible until the queue is under load.

Toolset

SQS Postgres Terraform

The author chose this stack — nobody handed it to them. If the choice is wrong, that is a verdict you can attach here.

Solution design

Drain, then promote. The old consumer stops taking new leases and finishes what it holds; the new one promotes only once the lease table is empty. No message is ever leased by both, because promotion is gated on the table rather than on a timer.

Lease table, as submitted leases(consumer_id, msg_id, leased_at, expires_at)

Alternatives rejected

Run both consumers briefly and deduplicate downstream: simpler cutover, but every consumer of the queue inherits the deduplication. Pause the queue for the swap: correct, and the backlog is unbounded at our arrival rate.

Judge whether the alternatives were considered fairly. A straw man here is a defect in the design.

What would prove this wrong

Any message processed twice during a cutover, at a 30-second lease TTL and peak arrival rate.

The author's own test. If it would not actually falsify the design, say so here — it is the sharpest verdict on the page.
