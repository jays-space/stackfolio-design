Peer review for engineers

A case study is an experiment. A verdict is a conclusion that survived scrutiny.

Engineers publish the reasoning behind their work — the challenge, the hypothesis, the design, the build — and peers matched to that niche review it. Nobody grades their own work here. Reading is open to everyone; signing in is only for taking part.

The loop

Learn something, document it, put it up for review. Each step sinks deeper.
01 Learn Take on a real problem — a swap you can see coming, a constraint you have to design around.
02 Document State the challenge, commit a hypothesis, then show the architecture, the solution design, the build.
03 Submit to peer review Reviewers in your niche are summoned. They ask, they challenge, and then they render a verdict with a reason attached.
