Elevation & layering

In-page separation is depth, not lift: three strata, a seam at every boundary, grain tightening as it sinks. Detachment from the page is the single exception.
--surface-1 · #242320 Working surface Present and editable. Loosest grain, brightest seam.
--surface-2 · #1B1A18 Settled Committed content: rows, cards, the body of a case.
--surface-3 · #131211 Context & history Page ground, provenance, older versions. Read on demand.

05

Radii

Flat by decision — a journal, not an app. There is no pill radius: chips are squared, and a pill shape is drift.
--radius-1 · 0
--radius-2 · 2
--radius-3 · 3
Assignment Surfaces and rows take radius-1. Controls, inputs and dialogs take radius-2. Chips and tally marks take radius-3, the largest the theme owns.
06

Strokes new

Three widths, each paired with a boundary colour. Width carries the meaning; colour only sets how loud it is.
--stroke-hair · 1px Dividers within a group; row seams. Pairs with boundary-2 or -3.
--stroke-edge · 2px The top edge of a section or overlay. Pairs with boundary-1, or accent when the surface is the subject.
--stroke-mark · 8px Status bars only. Wide enough that filled and hollow read apart; at 3px they do not.

Focus new

One ring, on every surface: --focus-ring in --confirm, offset 2px so the surface reads through it. The earlier accent ring was replaced — oxblood on surface-3 was not visible enough to be a focus indicator. Focus is never removed and never conveyed by colour change alone.

Opacity new

Two values, and they are the whole vocabulary for "not now" and "not important".
--op-disabled · 0.38 Applied to the whole control, never to its label alone. A disabled control keeps its layout and always has a reason next to it.
--op-muted · 0.62 Withdrawn review · kept for the record Superseded or withdrawn content that must stay readable. Never used on text already set in a dim role.

12

Control heights new

Three heights shared by every control that can be pressed or typed into, so a button and a field in the same row line up without either one being nudged.
--control-h-sm · 28 Dense rows and inline actions inside a list. Never the only way to perform an action on a page.
--control-h-md · 36 The default. Toolbars, forms, panels, anything on a working surface.
--control-h-lg · 44 One per screen at most: the committal action of a page or an overlay. Equals the hit-target minimum.
Hit target --hit-min: 44px is the floor for a pointer target. Controls drawn shorter than it — sm and md — are only permitted inside a container that supplies the rest of the target: a 44px row, a toolbar, a list item. A control below the floor with nothing around it is a defect, not a size choice.
Dialog · z 400 Surface-1, accent edge, scrim beneath The only shadow the system allows: long, low and colourless, and always with a scrim. Depth still does the work — the dialog is drawn on the working surface, one stratum above the page it covers.
