Component · Badge

A word in a box, kept neutral so the domains can colour it

Badge is the label that track, provenance, conclusion and niche are all built from. It carries no meaning of its own: three emphases, two sizes, an optional leading mark. Everything a domain badge adds is colour and a word.

Emphasis

Removable

The one interactive form, and it exists for exactly one job: an applied filter the reader can drop. A badge that states a fact — a track, a verdict, a niche — is never removable.
Applied filters · live
No filters applied
The close control is 14px at sm and 16px at md, well under the hit minimum. It is allowed because it sits inside a filter row that is pointer-only and duplicated by a Reset action; on touch layouts the row is replaced by a list of Buttons.
Both sizes, all three emphases
Removable trims the right padding so the control sits closer to the edge than the label does, and the whole badge stays one box — no separate chip, no divider.
