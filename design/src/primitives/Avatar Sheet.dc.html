Component · Avatar

A square with one accent edge, whatever it is holding

Avatar identifies a person in a header or beside their work. It keeps the square of the rest of the system — no circles, no rings — and the accent edge on the left is what marks it as a person rather than a thumbnail. Three sizes, four contents, one shape.
